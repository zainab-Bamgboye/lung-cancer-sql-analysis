Lung Cancer Patient Data Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a large lung cancer patient dataset using Microsoft SQL Server to identify patterns in patient demographics, cancer stages, smoking status, and survival outcomes.

The project demonstrates my ability to use SQL for healthcare data analysis, data quality assessment, aggregation, and insight generation.

🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering key healthcare-related questions:

* What is the demographic distribution of the patients?
* What is the average age of the patients?
* How are patients distributed across cancer stages?
* How does smoking status vary across the patient population?
* What proportion of patients survived?
* How does survival vary by gender and cancer stage?
* What is the relationship between smoking status and survival?
* Are there missing, invalid, or potentially problematic data values?

🔍 Analysis Performed

1. Data Overview

* Examined the structure and contents of the dataset.
* Counted the total number of patients and columns.
* Reviewed sample records.

2. Data Quality Assessment

* Checked for missing values.
* Identified invalid age values.
* Checked for invalid survival values.
* Investigated repeated age values.

3. Patient Demographics

* Analyzed patient distribution by gender.
* Calculated the average patient age.
* Created age groups to compare different age categories.

4. Cancer Stage Analysis

* Examined the distribution of patients across cancer stages.
* Compared survival outcomes across cancer stages.
* Identified patients with advanced-stage cancer.

5. Smoking & Survival Analysis

* Analyzed smoking status across the patient population.
* Examined survival among current smokers.
* Compared survival outcomes by smoking status.

6. Survival Analysis

* Calculated the overall survival rate.
* Compared survival rates by gender.
* Compared survival rates across cancer stages.
* Analyzed survival rates by smoking status.

📊 Key Findings

The dataset contains 890,000 patient records and includes information on demographics, smoking status, cancer stage, and survival outcomes.

The analysis identified:

* An approximately equal distribution of male and female patients.
* A relatively even distribution of patients across the four cancer stages.
* Differences in survival outcomes across demographic and clinical categories.
* Survival patterns that can be further explored using additional statistical and visualization techniques.

Note: Findings in this project are descriptive and should not be interpreted as clinical conclusions or evidence of causation.

💡 Healthcare Relevance

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of patient data that can be used to identify trends, monitor outcomes, and support evidence-based decision-making.

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw healthcare data into structured information that can support healthcare reporting, patient outcome analysis, and data-driven decision-making.
